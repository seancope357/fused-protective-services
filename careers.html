THE 5-STAGE VETTING PROTOCOL

Every candidate is evaluated through an exhaustive 5-stage verification process to ensure flawless tactical judgment, moral integrity, and client discretion.

01
STAGE 1: VERIFICATION

TOPS License & Credential Verification

We review your professional resume, Texas Online Private Security (TOPS) licensing history, and professional references within 48 business hours. Military and law enforcement service, or confirmed skilled civilian protective experience, is verified directly with prior employers.

🛡️ Gate Metric: Active Texas DPS Level II, III, or IV license confirmed; operational experience verified.
02
STAGE 2: INTEGRITY

Federal Fingerprint & Psychological Exam

Exhaustive background investigation covering criminal records, driving history, credit check, 10-panel drug screening, and MMPI-2 psychological evaluation for armed/PPO roles.

🛡️ Gate Metric: Zero felony convictions, clean drug panel, passed psychological screening.
03
STAGE 3: PROFICIENCY

Live Range & Situational Evaluation

Live-fire firearms qualification demonstrating sidearm proficiency, rapid failure clearing, shoot/no-shoot decision making, and physical fitness demonstration.

🛡️ Gate Metric: 90%+ range score, verified defensive tactics competence.
04
STAGE 4: ETHICS

Executive Interview with Cameron Harrell

An in-person command board interview evaluating communication presence, situational de-escalation instincts, tactical composure, and alignment with Fused standards.

🛡️ Gate Metric: Direct sign-off from Chief of Operations Cameron Harrell.
05
STAGE 5: ACTIVE DUTY

Encrypted Comms Sync & Post Orders

Issuance of tactical callsigns, AES-256 radio assignment, client post orders briefing, and scheduled deployment on active executive or commercial details.

🛡️ Gate Metric: Fully badged, deployed, and operational.
