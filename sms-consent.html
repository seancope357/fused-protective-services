Program description

By ticking the SMS consent box on our quote form, or by agreeing with a member of our team, you consent to receive text messages from Fused Protective Services about your security details and invoices: confirmations, day-before reminders, and payment notices.

Consent is not a condition of purchase. Message frequency varies with your engagement; typically one to three messages around each scheduled detail.

Opting out

Reply STOP at any time to stop receiving messages. Reply START to resume. Reply HELP for help, or call (512) 555-0199.

Costs and carriers

Message and data rates may apply. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Privacy

Your phone number is used only for the messages described here and is handled under our Privacy Policy. We do not share mobile numbers with third parties for their marketing.
