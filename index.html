Frequently Asked Questions

How rapidly can Fused deploy officers in Texas?
For planned contracts and events, we prefer 24–48 hours advance notice to run proper site recon and operational plans. For emergency or immediate threats, our rapid tactical dispatch team can deploy commissioned officers in under 45 minutes in the greater Austin and San Antonio areas.
Which areas of Texas does Fused operate in?
Our primary areas of operation are Austin and San Antonio, including the I-35 corridor and the Texas Hill Country between them. We also staff details in Dallas, Fort Worth, and Houston, and deploy statewide for executive travel and dedicated contracts.
What is the difference between Level 3 Armed and Level 4 PPO?
Under Texas DPS regulations, Level III Commissioned officers are uniformed armed security guards trained for asset defense, property patrol, and high-visibility deterrence. Level IV Personal Protection Officers (PPO) undergo advanced close-protection, executive escort, and covert concealment training to operate as plainclothes bodyguards for VIPs.
What uniform profiles are available for our venue?
We customize our presentation to match your venue's atmosphere: Executive Plainclothes (Black Suit & Tie), Discreet Tactical (Black Branded Polo & Khakis), or Full High-Visibility Uniform with marked protective vests and tactical duty belts.
What commercial insurance coverage do you maintain?
Fused Protective Services carries comprehensive commercial general liability insurance ($2,000,000+ aggregate), armed liability riders, and workers’ compensation coverage exceeding all Texas state statutory requirements. Certificates of Insurance (COI) naming your organization as additional insured are provided upon contract execution.
