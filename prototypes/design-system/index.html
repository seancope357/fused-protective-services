Tactical Luxury has two registers.

One gold spectrum, one spacing scale, one set of easings — two ways of speaking. Architectural asserts authority. Editorial, proven on /aerial, carries narrative and reads as more expensive because it withholds. This page renders from the real css/site.css, so it cannot drift.

The two registers

Architectural

Uncompromising
Protection

Use for: the brand plate, hero wordmark, division headers — anywhere the page must assert authority. Cinzel 900, uppercase, gold fills.

Editorial

A higher perspective.

Use for: narrative — a section's opening statement, a chapter, a quiet CTA. Outfit 400, sentence case, negative tracking, hairline gold.

The one hard rule: never raise --display-weight to make an editorial headline feel stronger. Scale and surrounding air do that job. Weight is exactly what turns restraint into shouting.

Do not mix the registers inside a single block. A Cinzel 900 uppercase heading above Outfit 400 body is correct; an editorial headline with a gold-filled slab button beneath it is not.

--gradient-gold-brushed  ·  decorative only

Full seven-stop sheen. Keep for decoration with no text on top: the .btn-outline hover wipe and the forge HUD rule. Dark text over its last two stops measures 3.99:1 and 2.93:1.

--gradient-gold-brushed-ui  ·  anything read or clicked

Same sheen, clamped so no stop falls below --logo-gold-core. Worst stop 5.59:1 — passes 4.5:1 everywhere. Resolves the open ui-standards.md Part E item 2 decision.
